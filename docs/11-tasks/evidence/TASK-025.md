## 3. Readings and technical choices

1. **The audit action of a mark.** SCHEMA section 5.8 named it `COST_CORRECTION_REFUSED`; INTERACTION_CONTRACTS section 8, the list of audit action codes, names `COST_INCOMPLETE_MARK`. The code uses the list's, and SCHEMA section 5.8 now says so (EXC-81: a name, no business meaning).
2. **The timeouts of a replay.** PostgreSQL's `transaction_timeout` ends the session, and `CommandTransaction` answers the lost connection SERVICE_BUSY, keeping its failure as the refusal's previous. Once the product's guard was held, `CorrectCost::refused()` reads the budget's end there (`SqlState::budgetEnded`) wherever the transaction met it — during the replay or its publication, at the result's write or at the commit — and writes the mark `TIME_BUDGET` on a new connection before REPLAY_REJECTED is answered (TR-TIME-01). Any other lost connection stays SERVICE_BUSY with no mark, since its commit may be unknown (TR-RETRY-03). The tests end a session of one second during the replay, at a statement after the result's write, and idle before the commit. Until fix cycle 1 (section 10) only a failure inside the work was read so, and the end after the work returned was recorded as review NOTE R-14. A statement of the replay or of its publication that meets the runtime role's statement timeout (10 seconds) is a timeout of the replay too (SCHEMA section 5.8): REPLAY_REJECTED with its mark `TIME_BUDGET` (`SqlState::statementTimedOut`; review finding R-04).
3. **The replay's start** is the state just before the source's sequence, read from the product's last zero point at or before it: the quantity, the increases at their effective cost and the projections of the issues since then, which is the moving average's own state (ADR-008 section 4.1). A refused history — an issue beyond the quantity at its point, an end that is not the stock on hand, a value out of range — is `UNSAFE_HISTORY`.
4. **The budget** is the heavy transaction budget less a reserve of 5 seconds for the publication (`config/costing.php`), checked before each movement and before the publication.
5. **The order of the refusals** under the guard is RC-15's: STALE_VERSION before POSTING_BLOCKED; neither starts the replay nor writes a mark (D-063).
6. **The mark's audit event** has no command identity: the refused command's identity rolled back with its transaction, and the mark's transaction is not a command of its own.
7. **STK-13.** RC-15 is the Costing module's first command, and its replay reads the balances and the posting blocks: STACK_AND_MODULES section 2 records Costing's dependency on Commands and its reads of Inventory.
8. **UXI-021** "Biaya perlu dilengkapi" is registered in the Laporan sub-navigation; the shell shows it once the Laporan destination exists (TASK-041). Until then the worklist is reached from RP-505's "Data biaya belum lengkap" and by its address.
9. **The worklist** ("UNKNOWN cost sources and marked products", ROUTE_CONTRACTS RP-503) lists each source whose effective cost is UNKNOWN and each source with an active mark, so that a marked product appears by the sources that carry its marks, each row naming its product and leading to its correction and to the product's RP-505; newest first by the time the cost was recorded, keyset-paged. RP-505 bounds its sources and corrections to the newest 100 each, the older being reached through the stock history.
10. **Without an opener.** NAVIGATION_CONTRACTS names the openers of UXP-504 (UXP-503, UXP-505, UXP-406, UXP-403) and of UXP-505 (UXP-302, UXP-503, UXP-006), which `from_page` carries; the client resolves "the page that opened it" from `from_page` and the referrer, since RC-15's envelope cannot carry it. A page opened by its bare address goes back to the first opener, UXP-503 or UXP-302; after its success, UXP-504 opened so leads to RC-15's result location, RP-505 of the product (RC-15 "else RP-505"; corrected after review NOTE R-10).
11. **The measured replay time** (contract section 8) is written by two tests on their error stream: the recomputation alone of the reference cap, 10,000 movements (`ValuationReplayTest`), and a whole correction whose replay covers 200 movements (`CorrectCostTest`), each on the CI runner's containers, not a PERF01 pass (BR-COST-28). Section 5 records the figures.
12. **No mark for an intent already committed** (fix cycle 1). The mark's transaction takes the product's guard, which the refused attempt held, so that attempt has ended when it reads the intent's identity: an identity there means the intent committed after all — which a session ended at its commit could hide — or was fenced since, and it writes no mark and the answer stays SERVICE_BUSY, so that the client's repeat reads the outcome (SCHEMA section 5.8, a mark "only then"; TR-RETRY-03; ADR-007 sections 3 and 4). The commit of a correction is thus read from the committed records, never guessed from the message that ended its session. The check sees what has committed when the mark's transaction holds the guard: a repeat of the same intent whose identity is not yet committed then may still commit after the mark, which its correction clears at once, the first request answered REPLAY_REJECTED and the repeat COMMITTED, as before this cycle (verification NOTE V3-05).

Without it, a mark written after a reversal would stay active.

Fix cycle 1 does not widen it: the result's write and the commit carry no user text, and `mark()`'s check of the intent's identity bounds its effect.
